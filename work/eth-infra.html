Case Study 05

ETH Infra

Haridwar, Queensfield & Rishikesh One Digital / EDM / Social 2024

The Brief

255 assets across the engagement

Client — ETH Infra · Role — Graphic Designer · 2024

Multi-project launch suite across three distinct locations, each with its own design personality sharing one brand framework.

Project goal

Create launch and ongoing campaign material for three ETH projects simultaneously: Rishikesh One (spiritual tourism investment), Queensfield (family-oriented), and Haridwar One — each targeting different buyer demographics.

Design decision

A shared ETH brand framework (dark blue base, gold ornamental accents, serif typography) adapted per project: Rishikesh One leaned classical/ornate, Queensfield went clean and family-friendly, Haridwar One emphasized investment value.

Approach

Design decisions
01

One framework, three expressions

The ETH brand identity (dark blue + gold + serif type) stayed consistent across all three projects, but each location got distinct imagery, messaging tone, and accent treatments tailored to its buyer profile.

02

EDM as primary channel

With multiple projects launching simultaneously, investor EDMs became the workhorse format — each one designed to function as a standalone pitch with pricing, USPs, and a clear call to action in a single scroll.

03

Festival content as continuity

Between major campaign pushes, festival and occasion-based content (Independence Day, Janmashtami) maintained brand visibility while the next launch EDM was being designed.
