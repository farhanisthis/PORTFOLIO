Campaign design · Panoramic social

Trevoc

A real estate campaign framed like a film.

Panoramic social designs and streaming-inspired teasers for a residential launch in Gurugram.

My contribution
Campaign layouts, panoramic social grids, and teaser creative
Agency work period
May–September 2026

The brief

Give a residential launch a distinct visual identity across social covers and digital teasers.

Design approach

The Art of Stillness uses wide compositions, quiet photography, and serif type. The teaser series switches to bold, streaming-inspired headlines while keeping the composition restrained.

Across formats

Compare the long-form social compositions with the teaser cards to see how the same campaign adapts to two different ways of getting attention.
