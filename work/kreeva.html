Case Study 03

Kreeva

Bespoke ultra-luxury residences Brand Narrative / Social / Illustration 2024

The Brief

184 assets across the engagement

Client — Kreeva · Role — Graphic Designer · 2024

Crafting a brand story through illustration and narrative design, not just photography.

Project goal

Build Kreeva's brand narrative through a series of social campaigns that communicated brand values, team culture, and the craftsmanship behind bespoke luxury residences.

Design decision

A crimson-and-gold palette with line illustration and icon-driven storytelling — deliberately distinct from the photography-heavy approach used for other luxury clients. The illustration style made abstract brand values tangible.

Approach

Design decisions
01

Illustration over photography

For a brand built on craftsmanship and bespoke values, hand-drawn line illustrations communicated care and intentionality in a way stock photography couldn't match.

02

Crimson & gold identity

A rich crimson base with gold accent created a distinctive, ownable colour territory that stood apart from the cool blues and warm beiges of competing luxury brands.

03

Series-based storytelling

The Friendship Day campaign told a multi-part story across carousel posts — each slide revealing a different "rare pair" behind the Kreeva brand, building narrative over swipes.
