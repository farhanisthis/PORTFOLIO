Case Study 04

Elevate Vanya

Luxury 3 BHK residences, Dehradun Digital / Print / Outdoor 2024

The Brief

142 assets across the engagement

Client — Elevate Infracon, Dehradun · Role — Graphic Designer · 2024

Selling a hillside lifestyle, not just square footage — through warm photography and aspirational outdoor advertising.

Project goal

Launch campaign for a premium Dehradun residential project positioned around hill views, proximity to Doon School, and a 16,000 sq ft clubhouse — across social, print standees, and outdoor hoarding.

Design decision

Warm sunset-hour photography became the visual signature. Every piece — from grid covers to 6-foot standees — opened with the same golden-hour architectural render, building instant recognition across touchpoints.

Approach

Design decisions
01

Golden-hour as brand signature

The warm sunset architectural render became the hero across every format — grid covers, standees, and digital banners all opened with the same golden-hour moment, creating instant visual recognition.

02

Lifestyle over specification

"Why chase the view when you can own it?" — the headline strategy led with aspiration rather than BHK counts and pricing, positioning Vanya as a lifestyle choice rather than a real estate transaction.

03

Print at architectural scale

6-foot standees and outdoor hoarding demanded compositions that read from 20 feet away. Large type, high-contrast renders, and minimal copy ensured the brand landed at scale.
