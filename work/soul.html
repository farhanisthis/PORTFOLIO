Case Study 05

Soul / Vankriti

Eco-Luxury Forest Living & Villa Plots 12K Panoramic Grid / Direct EDMs / B2B Network 2024

The Brief

275 assets across the engagement

Client — Soul / Vankriti · Role — Graphic Designer · 2024

Biophilic luxury: establishing an organic residential sanctuary across massive 12K panoramic feeds and rapid B2B broker acquisition.

Project goal

Position Soul / Vankriti as a premier nature-immersed luxury plot community, driving retail buyer interest through immersive social panoramas while concurrently building a motivated Channel Partner (CP) distribution network.

Design decision

A serene biophilic palette — forest canopy greens, deep earth tones, and warm natural timber — composited across colossal 12,960×5,400 pixel panoramic grid canvases. This visual weight was paired with rapid 9:16 WhatsApp nurture mailers for direct conversions.

Approach

Design decisions
01

12K Panoramic compositing

Mastered colossal multi-gigabyte Photoshop compositions (`soul grid-1.psd`), blending drone canopy photography with 3D architectural renders so that 9 individual Instagram feed tiles formed one seamless landscape.

02

Direct WhatsApp nurture engine

Built a sequential 7-part EDM series optimized for mobile screens. Each asset highlighted a distinct lifestyle facet — organic orchards, wellness decks, and site accessibility — with a clear call to action.

03

B2B Channel Partner mobilization

Crafted broker acquisition collateral with bold commission structures, clear RERA compliance, and event invitations to rapidly scale on-ground sales velocity.
